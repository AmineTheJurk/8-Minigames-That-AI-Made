Classic Games Gallery

I Asked AI to make some classic games. And these were the results, ENJOYYYYY!

Eight little games, all played in the browser.

Pong

Play against the computer. First to 7 points wins.

Memory Match

Flip two cards at a time and find every matching pair in as few moves as you can.

Brick Breaker

Bounce the ball off your paddle, clear every brick, and keep your three lives.

Snake

Steer with arrows, WASD or swipes. Eat food to grow, and don't bite your own tail.

Typing Test

Type a passage for 30 or 60 seconds and see your words per minute and accuracy live.

Harmonograph

Four swinging pendulums draw fading loops. Shape them with sliders or hit Random.

Life Garden

Conway's Game of Life. Draw cells by hand or start from a pattern and watch it grow.

Zombie Wave

Top-down shooter. Waves of zombies close in from every edge, so aim well and reload.
